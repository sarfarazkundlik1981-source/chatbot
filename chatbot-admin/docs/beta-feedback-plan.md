# Beta Feedback Plan

## Goal

Validate real-world usability with a small group of businesses before broad rollout.

## Beta Cohort

Target 3 to 5 businesses with different usage profiles:

- simple brochure site
- service business with lead capture focus
- business with Elementor-heavy landing pages
- business with frequent FAQ updates

## What To Observe

- how often owners update knowledge entries
- whether branding and widget settings are understandable without help
- whether unanswered questions are useful enough to maintain
- whether plugin setup is confusing
- whether lead notifications are actionable
- whether Elementor trigger modes match real usage

## Beta Review Questions

- Was setup completed without developer help?
- Which dashboard area was confusing?
- Which answers from the bot were most often wrong or incomplete?
- Did lead collection feel natural or intrusive?
- Was the widget behavior acceptable on mobile?
- Did the plugin update/compatibility messaging make sense?

## Weekly Beta Loop

1. Collect support issues and usage observations.
2. Group findings into:
   - setup friction
   - answer quality
   - dashboard UX
   - widget UX
   - operational issues
3. Prioritize fixes by repeated impact, not by anecdote.
4. Ship small improvements weekly.
5. Re-test the affected workflow with the same beta users.

## Exit Criteria

Move beyond beta when:

- onboarding succeeds without manual engineering intervention
- plugin verification and widget rendering are stable
- lead capture works consistently
- unanswered-question workflow is being used
- no critical support issue repeats across multiple beta clients
